## 2. The batch problem — `<StaggerC>` · white

- **Chips:** ["The problem"]
- **Headline:** Trainer hours don't stretch to a whole cohort.
- **Cards:**
  1. **Too many students, too few trainers.** A handful get mock rounds. The rest walk in cold.
  2. **No early warning.** You find out who wasn't ready after they've been rejected.
  3. **Uneven prep.** Practice depends on who booked time, not who needed it most.
  4. **No picture for leadership.** Hard to show readiness or improvement across a batch.

## 3. Readiness scale — `<TiersJ>` · grey

- **Chips:** ["Readiness at a glance"]
- **Headline:** Three states, every student sorted.
- **Panels (staggered):**
  1. **Ready.** Cleared the bar on skills and interview performance. Keep them warm.
  2. **Needs work.** Close, with specific gaps. Targeted remediation, then re-test.
  3. **At risk.** Not yet ready. Prioritise trainer time here, where it moves the needle.
- **Left note:** Every student is placed on the scale from their mock scores, and moves as they improve.
