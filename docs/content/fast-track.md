## 8. FAQ — `<DualK>` · white

1. **Is this a subscription?** No. It's a single one-time fee per Fast Track.
2. **What currency will I pay in?** AED in the UAE, INR in India. The price is shown before you pay.
3. **What does the refund cover?** If we don't review you within 72 hours, the fee is refunded.
4. **Does Fast Track guarantee a job?** No. It guarantees speed, a fair interview, feedback, and assets. Outcomes still depend on merit and the company's decision.
5. **Can strong candidates skip it?** Yes. The free path gives the same evaluation. Fast Track is for speed, prep, and reusable assets.
6. **Who can see my interview video?** Only the relevant hiring team, under access control and a retention limit. See the consent page.
