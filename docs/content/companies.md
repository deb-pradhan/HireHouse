## 8. FAQ — `<DualK>` · white

1. **Is it really free?** Yes. No licence, no setup, no per-seat cost to post roles and receive shortlists.
2. **How fast is the shortlist?** Days, not weeks, depending on applicant volume for the role.
3. **How are candidates ranked?** On skills and fit via an ELO-style merit score, then a structured interview. Never on keyword matching alone.
4. **Do we still make the decision?** Always. We hand you a verified shortlist; the hire is your call.
5. **What about agencies?** Agencies can pull from the pre-vetted pool to cut sourcing time per mandate. Commercial terms on request.
6. **Can we earn as a partner?** Yes. Opt in to refer companies or candidates and earn a share of revenue. See Partners.
