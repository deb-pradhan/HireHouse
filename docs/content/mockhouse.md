## 6. FAQ — `<DualK>` · white

1. **What's a JD-based mock?** You paste a real job description and get an interview shaped around it.
2. **Is there a free version?** Yes. A free path is always available; premium adds more rounds and depth.
3. **How is it scored?** On a consistent rubric, with written feedback on strengths and gaps.
4. **How does it relate to Fast Track?** Fast Track includes two MockHouse rounds so you prep before the real interview.
5. **Do institutions use the same product?** Yes, with batch onboarding, trainer override, and cohort reports. See Institutions.
6. **Are mocks recorded?** They can be, for feedback, with your consent and a retention limit. See the consent page.
